Lambert Lake

and the family it's named for

A ring-shaped lake with an island in the middle, in what is now Vadnais Heights, Minnesota. In 1849 Abraham Lambert bought the island from the United States. This is his family's story, from a farm across the river from Quebec in the 1640s to the creek that still runs where the lake used to be.

Maps from 1856 to today, period paintings and the family's own records: Eustache crossing the ocean, Abraham's four-year journey west, the island, and the lake slowly drained. Ends with every map of the lake to explore yourself.
