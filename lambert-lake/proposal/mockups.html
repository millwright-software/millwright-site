There was a lake here

Lambert Lake, as federal
surveyors mapped it, 1847

The lake in 1847Lambert Creek todayCity parks

In 1847 a federal survey crew walked the shore near here and mapped a ring-shaped lake of about 630 acres, with a 97-acre island in the middle. This park sits on the lake's north side, where the water was.

In 1849 Abraham Lambert, a French-Canadian farmer who had settled at Little Canada in 1845, bought the island from the United States. By 1867 the county map called the water Lamberts Lake.

Then the lake was drained: a water company's canal in 1870, a farmer's ditch in 1893, county ditches from 1916. House lots were sold from the 1930s. What is left runs out as Lambert Creek, to Vadnais Lake and St. Paul's drinking water.

Sources: U.S. General Land Office survey plat, T30N R22W, 1847–48, and land patents, 1849; Neill, History of Ramsey County, 1881; Ramsey County History, 1964; Swank v. Barnum, 1896; VLAWMO. Imagery: Esri. Draft panel, 36 × 24 in.
