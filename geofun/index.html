GeoFun

Every geocaching app is built for the adult doing the caching. GeoFun is built for the kid who wants to hold the phone.

Hand a kid the phone. It pings faster as they get closer, buzzes when they point it the right way, counts the feet down, and reads the clue out loud in words they know. When they find the box, they take the picture and earn a sticker on their adventure map. In the car, it pings when treasure is nearby.

Built for small hands

  • The clue is rewritten for kids. “Find the biggest oak tree you can see. Look at the bottom where flat stones are.”
  • It talks. The clue, the countdown, the cheer. A real voice, not a robot.
  • Finding is a hold, not a tap. A grown-up is beside them; the phone just makes sure.
  • A Brave Try sticker when they can’t find it, so a DNF is still a good day.
  • Two moods. Fun mode pings and chimes. Tracker mode is calm and sci-fi for the kid who’s outgrown chimes.

Built for grown-ups to trust

  • Every grown-up function sits behind a two-second hold and a multiplication problem.
  • No child accounts. The parent signs in. The kid never touches geocaching.com.
  • No ads, no analytics, no third-party anything. Photos never leave the app.
  • Kid guidance locks above walking speed. In the car, only the driver gets directions.
  • Honest about GPS. Inside the search zone it says “look around here” instead of pointing at a spot it can’t know.

Where it stands. A public preview of an app in field test, for families with children around four to eight. It imports GPX pocket queries today, and we are applying to the Geocaching API partner program so a parent can search and fetch hints in the app. Hints need a Geocaching Premium membership, so GeoFun is a Premium household’s app.

A TestFlight preview is coming. GeoFun is not affiliated with Geocaching HQ.
